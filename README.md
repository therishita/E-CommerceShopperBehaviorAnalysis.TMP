# E-Commerce Shopper Behavior Analysis (Amazon/Shopify Based)

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce shopper behaviour and lifestyle patterns using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). It aims to understand customer demographics, shopping preferences, spending habits, device usage, and purchasing behaviour through data-driven exploration.

The analysis is performed on a dataset containing **1,000,000 customer records and 60 features**, providing a large-scale view of e-commerce consumer behaviour.

## Problem Statement

E-commerce platforms generate vast amounts of customer data through browsing activities, purchasing decisions, and interactions with digital platforms. Understanding this data can help businesses identify customer preferences, recognize spending patterns, and make informed business decisions.

The objective of this project is to explore and analyze shopper behaviour using Python-based data analysis techniques. The project applies data cleaning, statistical analysis, univariate analysis, bivariate analysis, multivariate analysis, and temporal analysis to identify meaningful patterns, relationships, and trends within the dataset.

## Dataset Description

- **Dataset:** `e_commerce_shopper_behaviour_and_lifestyle.xlsx`
- **Total Records:** 1,000,000
- **Total Features:** 60
- **File Format:** Excel (.xlsx)
- **Data Source:** Provided for academic analysis.

The dataset contains information related to:
- Customer demographics
- Income and employment
- Education and lifestyle
- Device usage
- Shopping preferences
- Spending behaviour
- Premium subscriptions
- Social media influence
- Purchase-related information

## Objectives

- Understand the demographic distribution of e-commerce shoppers.
- Analyze customer spending patterns and shopping preferences.
- Examine relationships between different customer attributes.
- Identify important factors associated with high-value shoppers.

## Analysis Performed

The project covers the following stages:

1. **Data Understanding:** Exploring the dataset structure, dimensions, columns, and data types.
2. **Data Cleaning and Preprocessing:** Preparing the dataset for reliable analysis.
3. **Univariate Analysis:** Examining individual variables and their distributions.
4. **Bivariate Analysis:** Investigating relationships between pairs of variables.
5. **Multivariate Analysis:** Exploring interactions among multiple variables and profiling customer segments.
6. **Temporal Analysis:** Studying time-related patterns in customer purchasing behaviour.
7. **Statistical Analysis:** Applying statistical techniques to understand the underlying data.
8. **Business Insights:** Interpreting the findings and identifying their potential business implications.

## Summary of Findings

The exploratory analysis revealed several interesting patterns:

- **Device Usage:** Mobile devices account for the largest share of shoppers, representing approximately 60.02% of the dataset. Desktop users account for 29.98%, while tablet users represent 9.99%.
- **Geographical Distribution:** Brazil has the highest number of shoppers, with 100,476 records (approximately 10.05%).
- **Education:** Customers with a Bachelor's degree form the largest education category, accounting for 299,953 records (approximately 30%).
- **Employment:** The dataset shows a relatively balanced distribution across employment categories, with each category representing approximately 20% of the records.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Spending behaviour and customer characteristics were explored through multivariate analysis to understand different shopper segments.
- **Purchasing Trends:** Temporal analysis was conducted to investigate time-related patterns in shopper behaviour.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis can be used to extract meaningful information from a large e-commerce shopper dataset. By examining customer demographics, spending behaviour, shopping preferences, and purchasing patterns, the analysis provides a better understanding of consumer behaviour.

The findings can help e-commerce businesses identify important customer segments, understand device preferences, and make more informed decisions regarding customer engagement, marketing strategies, and business planning.
